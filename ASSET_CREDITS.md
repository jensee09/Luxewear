# Fashion image credits

The storefront uses 13 real Pexels and Unsplash image-search photos, compressed to WebP; all local source images are below the planning document's 150 KB limit. Product names and SKU data are demo catalog content. Images are representative editorial/lifestyle references, not verified photos of exact supplier garments. On a product-detail page the assigned representative image is shown first; any additional frames are styling references curated by clothing category, not different views of the exact same garment.

The retained search history contains provider labels, subjects, and local image files, but not the individual original source-page URLs or photographer names. Confirm each photo's original license and required photographer credit before any commercial launch; this list is not commercial-use clearance.
